A puzzle that looks simple — until the clock starts.

So I booted up Block Out for the first time, and honestly? Looked simple enough - you've got some colored blocks, matching gates, and a board that doesn't seem too intimidating. The goal is clear. Slide each block to the gate with the same color.

But once the timer begins ticking, the puzzle transforms.

That's when every move starts to count. Mess up one block and you might trap another one. What looked like a simple path suddenly turns into this crazy maze. Suddenly you are not just moving shapes — you are planning routes, predicting obstacles, and trying to stay one step ahead of the board.

What starts as a calm puzzle quickly becomes a focused mental challenge.

Move with purpose.

Really, Block Out comes down to thinking spatially and planning your moves carefully. Each level presents a grid filled with blocks of different colors and shapes. Somewhere on that grid are matching gates waiting to receive them.

Pretty basic stuff - just get each block to its matching gate.

But there’s a twist. The paths between blocks and gates rarely stay open for long. Moving one block can block another. A route that looked obvious may suddenly collapse into a puzzle that needs rethinking. That's when you realize you actually need to think about this stuff.

You'll do way better if you look at the whole board instead of just obsessing over one block at a time.
